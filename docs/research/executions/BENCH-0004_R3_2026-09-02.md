## 1. Headline result

**The broad form of CLM-0004 is FALSIFIED at this scope.**

The combined temporal + relationship + provenance arm (D4) did not merely fail to
beat the semantic + metadata baseline (C). It performed **substantially worse**:

```text
C  (semantic + metadata)      chain coverage@3 = 0.7292
D4 (all three mechanisms)     chain coverage@3 = 0.5938     -0.1354
```

No individual mechanism reached the predeclared +0.05 effect threshold. The
largest positive effect measured anywhere in this experiment was produced by
**plain metadata weighting** (C over B, +0.0625) — which is precisely the
alternative explanation that the BENCH-0004 case definition named as its
falsifier: *"improvement disappears after controlling for indexing/metadata
quality."*

## 6. Why the structural mechanisms failed — hub amplification

The relationship arm's failure has an identifiable and generalisable cause.

One-step score propagation adds score to a document in proportion to how
**connected** it is, not how **relevant** it is. In a governance corpus, the most
connected documents are the ones that reference everything: `CLAIM_INVENTORY`
(in-weight 1.55), `BENCH_CASES` (1.32), `CONSTITUTION` (1.31), `COLD_REVIEW`
(1.27), `SESSION_STATE` (1.18). These are also the *least specific* answers to
any particular question.

The neutral control makes the damage visible. On N1 ("What fields are required in
a minimum APF decision record?"):

```text
C   top-3:  DECISIONS*  CORPUS_MAP  HOTL          coverage 1.00
D2  top-3:  README      MASTER      CORPUS_MAP    coverage 0.00
```

The single correct document is displaced out of the top 3 by three generic hubs.

**Connectivity in a governance corpus is anti-correlated with answer
specificity.** Documents earn edges by cross-referencing many claims and
benchmarks, which is exactly what a document does when it is an index rather
than an answer.

Critically, this is *not* a claim that the derived graph is noise. D2 is the one
arm that **passes F3**, at the 99.5th percentile of its own null distribution:
the mechanically derived graph is decisively better than a degree-preserving
random rewiring. Both facts hold simultaneously and must be reported together:

> The relationship structure is real. One-step score propagation is the wrong way
> to consume it.

Where D2 does help, it helps exactly where predicted — R1 (+0.33) and R3 (+0.33),
the claim → benchmark → execution chain questions. It simply pays more elsewhere
than it earns there.

### A secondary structural finding: gating

D1 and D3 are gated by query cue tokens and correctly abstain on all four neutral
questions; D2 applies unconditionally. This maps exactly onto the damage: the two
gated arms never harm the neutral class, and the ungated arm costs it −0.25.

```text
gate firing:  temporal 6/16 queries,  provenance 6/16 queries,  relationship 16/16
```

The temporal gate fired on all four TEMPORAL questions and the provenance gate on
all four PROVENANCE questions, so neither arm is untested — they were exercised
on their intended cases and produced no gain there.

D1 reordered the top-3 on 5 of 16 questions while leaving mean coverage exactly
unchanged: temporal weighting **moves documents without recovering evidence**.

## 7. Consequence for Round 2

Round 2 reported D beating C by +0.208 chain coverage@2 using a **hand-specified**
relationship graph, with the operator who wrote the questions also drawing the
edges. Round 3 changed exactly that variable — edges derived mechanically from
filename mentions and shared identifiers — and enlarged the corpus from 7 to 21
documents. The reported advantage did not shrink; it **reversed** (−0.104 for the
same mechanism in isolation).

The most economical explanation of the R1/R2/R3 sequence:

```text
R1  no gain            (document-level recall, 4 docs)
R2  large gain         (hand-specified graph, 7 docs)
R3  reversal           (mechanically derived graph, 21 docs)
```

R2's positive result is therefore best explained by **operator construction of
the graph**, not by relationship awareness as a retrieval mechanism. Round 2's
§8 "provisional state: SUPPORTED within this pilot scope" no longer stands and is
superseded by this round. This is recorded as an explicit revision rather than a
silent correction, per `CLAIM_RECONCILIATION` anti-drift rule 7.

## 9. What this round does NOT establish

Symmetry with the predeclaration's limit 1 requires stating the negative result's
bounds as plainly as a positive one's:

1. This does **not** show that temporal, provenance or relationship retrieval is
   useless in general. It falsifies CLM-0004 **on a 21-document APF governance
   corpus with n = 16 questions and prototype mechanisms**.
2. The mechanisms tested are deliberately simple (one-step propagation, cue-gated
   recency, provenance-field cosine). A production graph or vector implementation
   could behave differently. What has been falsified is the proposition that
   these mechanisms deliver a **free win over a competent metadata baseline**.
3. Answer-level grounding, unsupported-claim rate and answer utility — three
   declared BENCH-0004 primary measures — remain **unmeasured** after three
   rounds. No answer generation was performed.
4. Per-class results are n = 4 and carry no statistical power. They are
   directional only.
5. The corpus remains APF dogfood material, not an independent engineering corpus.
6. Nothing here authorises an architecture decision. Per `CLAIM_RECONCILIATION`,
   that additionally requires counter-evidence review, scope update and an
   explicit human decision record.

That said, the burden of proof sits with the claim. CLM-0004 has now been
attacked three times and has produced one positive result that is explained by
operator bias in graph construction.

## 10. Deviations from the predeclaration

Recorded as deviations rather than corrected silently, per the predeclaration's
standing instruction.

**D-1 — latency measurement defect.** In the first execution, `Engine.score()`
computed the C score before the `arm == "B"` early return, so arm B performed C's
work and discarded it. This inflated B's latency only. The ordering was fixed and
the harness re-run. All coverage, null-model, α-sweep and sensitivity outputs are
**bit-identical before and after the fix** (verified by direct JSON comparison);
only the latency table changed. §5 reports the corrected numbers.

**D-2 — provenance normalisation sensitivity.** The predeclaration specified the
provenance signal as a raw cosine while the other two signals are min-max
normalised, which gives provenance less influence at equal α. The predeclared
sensitivity check was run: with the provenance signal min-max normalised, D3 =
0.7292 (Δ vs C **+0.0000**) and D4 = 0.5938 (unchanged). The conclusion is not an
artefact of this asymmetry.

**No other deviations.** The corpus, task set, required-evidence labels, cue
lists, α values, seed and falsifier thresholds are exactly as committed in
`88aed05`. The `TRACE_MAP` citation-artifact defect declared in advance had no
observable effect, as predicted.

## 11. Next attack

The claim states above are what the current evidence supports. The productive
next moves, in priority order:

1. **Do not promote any part of CLM-0004.** Do not select a graph database or
   relationship-aware retrieval layer as an APF contract on the current evidence.
2. **Test consumption, not just structure.** D2 passing F3 while losing to
   baseline says the graph carries signal that one-step propagation destroys.
   Worth testing: hub-penalised propagation (inverse-degree weighting), edge-type
   restriction, or using relations to *expand a result set* after ranking rather
   than to *reweight ranking*. This is a new, narrower hypothesis and needs its
   own predeclaration.
3. **Measure what has never been measured.** Answer grounding and
   unsupported-claim rate are declared BENCH-0004 primary measures still
   untouched after three rounds. Document-level coverage may simply be the wrong
   dependent variable for the claim.
4. **Escape the dogfood corpus.** Every round so far has used APF's own
   governance documents, whose hub topology is likely unusual. An independent
   engineering corpus with real revision/configuration transitions is required
   before any generalisation in either direction.
5. **Proceed to BENCH-0006.** Provenance value for *evidence reuse and
   reproduction* (CLM-0006) is a different claim from provenance value for
   *retrieval ranking* (CLM-0004b). This round says nothing about it, and it is
   next in the declared execution order.
