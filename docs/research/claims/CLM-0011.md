## Predeclared falsifiers

Fixed before execution. The primary metric is **complete chain@5 on chain tasks (T1+T2)**, compared against condition C by paired bootstrap over tasks.

**F1 — primary.** D4 does not exceed C on complete@5, or the paired bootstrap 95% interval for (D4 − C) includes zero.
→ CLM-0011 is **falsified on CORPUS-0001**. It is not rescued by reporting a different k, a different metric, or a parameter sweep.

**F2 — mechanism attribution.** D4 exceeds C but D2 alone does not, i.e. the gain is carried by D1 (temporal) or D3 (provenance).
→ The word *relation* in the statement is wrong. The claim is rewritten to name the mechanism that actually carried it, and the relation version is recorded as CONTRADICTED.

**F3 — no headroom.** C alone reaches complete@5 ≥ 0.90 on chain tasks.
→ The task set cannot discriminate. Result is **INCONCLUSIVE**, not supportive, regardless of any margin.

**F4 — regression cost.** A condition that beats C on chain tasks loses more than 5 percentage points against C on the T0 control (single-document retrieval, hit@5).
→ The advantage was bought by damaging ordinary retrieval. The claim may be recorded as supported only with that cost stated, and is not promotable under DEC-0001 until the cost is addressed.

## Preregistered analysis constraints

1. Parameters `ALPHA=0.30`, `BETA=0.50`, `GAMMA=0.30` are fixed in `run.py` and were not tuned. A post-hoc sweep may be reported as sensitivity analysis only; it cannot change the primary verdict.
2. `SEED=20260902` fixes the T0 control sample and the bootstrap.
3. The primary verdict uses k=5. Results at k=3 and k=10 are reported for completeness and are not the decision metric.
4. Ground truth uses only header-declared relations. The D2 graph uses only body-text references. Their overlap is reported, not assumed.
5. If the run produces a result not covered by F1–F4, that gap is recorded as a defect in this record rather than resolved in favour of the claim.

## What a positive result would and would not authorize

A pass on all four falsifiers supports CLM-0011 **as worded**, on one corpus, at the retrieval stage.

It does not clear DEC-0001. Exit criterion 1 is satisfied by CORPUS-0001, and criterion 2 by this record, but criterion 3 requires the promoted wording to match the tested scope — retrieval-stage chain recovery on a structured, relation-bearing corpus in a non-target domain — and criterion 4 requires a separate decision record. Benchmark rule B6 additionally requires a second corpus before the mechanism generalizes.

## Post-execution amendment — 2026-09-02

Added after BENCH-0004 Round 3. Everything above this line is the pre-registration and is unchanged except `current_state`, `confidence`, `last_reviewed`, and appended `counter_evidence` entries. The falsifiers, mechanism, scope, and observable prediction as originally written are preserved verbatim so the amendment can be audited against them.

**Outcome.** All four predeclared falsifiers survived. `current_state` moves `TESTABLE` → `SUPPORTED`, on one corpus, at the retrieval stage, at k ≤ 5.

**Defect recorded per preregistered analysis constraint 5.** The run produced a result the falsifiers did not cover: `observable_prediction` was wrong. The margin over the baseline was +0.1228 on topically dissimilar pairs and +0.1250 on similar pairs — flat, where the record predicted it would be concentrated on the dissimilar half. The claim's *effect* held; the claim's *explanation* did not. Constraint 5 requires this be recorded as a defect rather than resolved in the claim's favour, so it is recorded here and in `counter_evidence` above.

**The `mechanism` field is wrong as written and is not silently corrected.** It says the two methods diverge where a related document is topically dissimilar. Round 3 section 6 shows what actually happens: the gain is +0.3171 on tasks where a relation edge was extracted and exactly 0.0000 where it was not, regardless of topical distance. The binding quantity is relation-extraction coverage, which was 36% on this corpus. Rewriting `mechanism` is deferred to the next benchmark preparation, per DEC-0002 option C, because the rewrite should be tested rather than asserted.

**Scope the wording still does not carry.** The statement above omits the k ≤ 5 bound, the edge-coverage dependency, and the control cost. Under DEC-0001 exit criterion 3 this is what currently blocks promotion, and it is criterion 3 rather than the evidence that blocks it.

**Gate status:** criteria 1 and 2 met, criteria 3 and 4 not met. Benchmark rule B6 replication not satisfied. See `docs/research/executions/BENCH-0004_R3/EXECUTION.md` section 10.
